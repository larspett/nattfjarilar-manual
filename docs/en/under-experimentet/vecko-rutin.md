# Weekly routine: putting out, emptying, reporting

## Sampling schedule

Sampling happens **once a week per site**, from when the trap is in place through to **30 September 2026**. Depending on weather, catches, and participants' interest, the season may be extended by a further month.

Traps were distributed to most participants during weeks 28-29; the rest will receive their traps soon. If you've received your trap and are at the site, put it out as soon as it suits you. There's no shared start date — start as soon as it's practically possible for you.

**Northern sites (Marsfjäll and further north):** during a period around midsummer, the night isn't dark enough for light catching. Calculated pause dates can be found in [Gradient (Lund-Abisko)](../hur-du-satter-ut/gradient-lund-abisko.md#light-pause-at-northern-sites). You're welcome to try even a little earlier than these dates too — all data is welcome and helps calibrate the calculations.

## Suitable weather conditions

- Temperature should be at least **+5°C**, preferably above +10°C — below that, moth activity is low regardless
- **Avoid windy nights** — risk of the trap tipping over, especially the lighter Finnish models; add stones or other weight to the bucket if needed
- **Avoid heavy rain** when putting out traps — electrical components and the powerbank are sensitive; if the trap gets wet inside, let it dry thoroughly before the next deployment
- Light rain during the night is normally not a problem for the moths
- **Cloudy nights with weak moonlight** are preferable to clear nights with a full moon — moonlight competes with the trap and reduces the catch

## What time of day?

### Putting out
Choose whichever time suits you practically. Traps with an automatic light sensor (LED-Emmer standard and the EntoLight models) can be put out before dark falls — the sensor switches the light on automatically. The LED-Emmer Quad lacks a sensor and stays lit continuously, so it's best put out at dusk. Always keep all light sensors outside the light buckets! The EntoLight models may work with the powerbank and sensor inside the trap, but for the sake of comparability — place all sensors outside the traps.

### Emptying traps
Empty the traps **in the morning, ideally before 10:00**, before it gets too warm and sunny. In warm weather, there's a greater risk that the moths are active and fly out when the trap is opened. We'll be providing nets that can be placed over the trap during emptying — these are expected to arrive in the first half of August. Try to report your observations the same day if possible.

## How many traps?

**All sites except Norrfjärden**: all **four traps should be out at their predetermined positions at the same time**. If that's not possible in a given week, get in touch at nattflyn@gmail.com for guidance. Trap placement and trap assignment are detailed in the separate site document for your location.

**Norrfjärden (10a)**: this site runs on an adapted rotating setup — one trap per night, alternating between the four project traps and the participant's own mercury-vapour trap at the same location. Each model is therefore sampled roughly every sixth night. The setup was adapted to the site's circumstances and resembles what was used during the NAT-PoMS sampling at this location.
