## Trap types

We won't be continuously reporting how well different traps perform relative to each other during the course of the trial, since that risks biasing the results. It's also not a given that the same trap performs the same way at different latitudes, or that "good" means the same thing in every context. A trap might be adequately effective in Skåne but insufficient in Norrbotten — or the other way around. Those questions are answered in the scientific analysis after the season.
