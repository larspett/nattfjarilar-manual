# Validation

Once you've reported your observations, they move on to validation. You're warmly welcome to help with validation yourself, both of your own observations and others'.

Interested in joining as a validator? Get in touch at [lars.pettersson@biol.lu.se](mailto:lars.pettersson@biol.lu.se).

We'll add information about how validation works here during August.

## Editing your own observations afterwards

See [Editing observations](../hur-du-rapporterar/andra-observationer.md).

## Further ahead

We're looking into the possibility of visualising data from the Indicia database, to give you as a participant more active feedback on what your particular trap has contributed. This isn't in place yet, but we expect to work on it after the summer holidays.
