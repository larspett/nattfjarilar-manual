# Editing an observation afterwards

Observations you've entered via the ButterflyCount app can be changed both in the app and on the website ([butterfly-monitoring.net](https://butterfly-monitoring.net/)), regardless of where they were originally registered. The same login is used in both places.

## In the app

Go to the survey, open the species you want to adjust under **Edit occurrence**. There you can change:

- **Count inside** / **Count outside** the trap
- **Image identifier** (who made the determination)
- **Comment**
- Add more photos of the same species

## On the website

Log in at [butterfly-monitoring.net](https://butterfly-monitoring.net/) with the same details as in the app. There you can find your survey (via the map or the list) and adjust counts the same way as in the app.

## Good to know

- Being able to edit afterwards is exactly why it's important **not** to register in training mode — see [Register your trap](registrera-falla.md).
- If you photograph the same animal twice, it counts as two individuals — adjust the count manually if needed.
- You can upload several photos in a batch for one trap; the app's classifier identifies them one by one afterwards.
- The app can't (yet) crop images. If you need to crop, take the photo with your phone's camera, crop it, and then upload it in the app.
- If the species identification is uncertain, feel free to try the apps [ObsIdentify](https://waarneming.nl/) and [Artsorakel](https://orakel.artsdatabanken.no/), which tend to be reliable for a quick check.

## Export to Artportalen

The goal is for data to be automatically synced to Artportalen, but that requires both your consent and approval from Artdatabanken, which isn't yet in place. Even now, though, you can always export your data and manually import it into Artportalen if you'd like it there too.
