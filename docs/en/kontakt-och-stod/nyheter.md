# News and experiences

This page collects both changes to the manual and practical experiences that come up during the season.

**Current version**: {{ site.version }} — {{ site.version_date }}

## When things go wrong

- Never register in training mode — see [Register your trap](../hur-du-rapporterar/registrera-falla.md)
- Always keep the light sensor **outside** the trap, not inside the bucket — applies to all models except the LED-Emmer Quad (which has no sensor)
- Powerbank won't start? See the powerbank troubleshooting guide and video under [Trap types](../falltyper/oversikt.md#powerbank-troubleshooting-mainly-relevant-for-the-quad-module)
- Suspect a technical fault in the app or on the website (not in this manual)? See [Report a technical issue](rapportera-tekniskt-fel.md) before proceeding on your own
