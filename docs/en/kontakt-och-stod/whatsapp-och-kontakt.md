## WhatsApp group

A WhatsApp group exists for quick exchange of experiences and support. Participation is voluntary.

[WhatsApp](https://www.whatsapp.com/) is an app for mobile phones that makes it easy to send chat messages to other users. It works well for group chat, which is why we use it as a support channel for the project. WhatsApp is owned by Meta, the parent company of Facebook. If you need help getting started, there's a good [guide to WhatsApp in Swedish](https://www.sv.se/storage/E8BC73B09D703A5E91AD0AC73CE8B096AB0D62F34E5E2399AEC71008ED452A9F/1f87bb6171ec4252b455316b4393e61d/pdf/media/9bc1c085e7b84114a724fbc1ed52699c/sv-whatsapp-guide-av-gabriel-eliasson.pdf).

Not already in the WhatsApp group but want to join? Send an email to [nattflyn@gmail.com](mailto:nattflyn@gmail.com).
